# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-09

CertGym ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the CertGym mobile app collects, uses, and stores your data.

---

## 1. Single Purpose

CertGym is a mobile app that helps users prepare for professional certification exams (such as tour guide licensing, financial certifications, AI/AWS certifications, civil service exams, and iPAS AI Solution Planner) through a quiz bank, flashcards, and a bookmarked-mistakes review system.

We do not collect any personal data beyond what is required for this single purpose.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

We **do not collect personally identifiable information (PII)**, financial, health, or authentication information. The app has no account or login system.

The only data the app processes is data you generate by using it:

- **Study progress**
  - Bookmarked questions and the collections you organize them into
  - Quiz history (score, date, category)
  - Level, XP, streaks, and unlocked badges
- **App preferences**
  - Selected language (Traditional Chinese / English)
  - Last-studied flashcard category/session/subject

> All data is stored **locally on your device**. Nothing is sent to external servers — CertGym has no backend and makes no network requests.

---

## 3. Permissions

CertGym does not request any sensitive device permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or similar). The app only reads and writes its own local storage on your device to save your study progress and preferences.

---

## 4. Remote Code, Ads & Analytics

CertGym **does not load or execute any remote code**, contains **no advertising SDKs**, and includes **no analytics or tracking libraries**. All code ships inside the app package.

---

## 5. Third-Party Sharing

We do **not sell, share, or transfer any user data to third parties**, because no user data ever leaves your device in the first place.

---

## 6. User Control

Users can:

- Delete any bookmarked question or collection at any time within the app.
- Clear practice records from the app's Records screen.
- Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.
